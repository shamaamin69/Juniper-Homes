Meet the founder

Shama Amin

A personal vision.

Founded by Shama Amin, Juniper Homes creates interiors that feel personal, elegant and thoughtfully considered. Based in Karachi, our studio brings together interior design and bespoke furniture, shaping spaces around the people who call them home.

Shama’s journey began with designing her own home. The response inspired a career in interiors, followed by a diploma from KLC School of Interior Design in London. Over more than 26 years, she has developed an approach that balances timeless aesthetics with the practical rhythms of everyday life.

Today, a team of over 20 skilled professionals, including artisans and an in-house manufacturing unit, brings that vision to life. Honesty, integrity and careful attention to each client’s brief guide our work, from the first conversation to the finishing details.
